Our story

A church family built on faith and fellowship.

Magena New Living SDA Church began in 2013 as a subsidiary congregation. Since its beginning, its story has been one of faith, worship and a desire to share the hope of Jesus Christ.

We honour the members and leaders who have helped shape our church, and we welcome every generation to take part in its continuing story.

Help us complete our history: church milestones, names of founding members, leadership timelines and special memories can be added as the congregation confirms them.
